# 📋 Section 03: Scrum Roles

## What You'll Learn

- Who does what in a Scrum team
- Why these three roles exist
- How roles fail when confused

## The Three Roles

1. **Product Owner** — Decides WHAT to build
2. **Scrum Master** — Enables the team
3. **Development Team** — Builds the product

## Why Separate Roles?

Think of building a house:

❌ **Bad:** One person decides design, builds it, and inspects it.
- Biased
- Doesn't catch own mistakes
- No accountability

✅ **Good:** Owner decides what they want, builder builds it, inspector checks it.
- Each person focuses on their job
- Mistakes get caught
- Clear accountability

**Same with software.**
